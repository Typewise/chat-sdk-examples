Northwind Analytics handbook

Everything you need to connect your data, build dashboards and get alerted when a metric moves. Open the assistant at any time: it knows which section you are reading.

Getting started

Connect a data source from Settings → Sources. Northwind supports Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake and CSV uploads. The first sync usually takes a few minutes; large warehouses can take up to an hour.

Invite teammates from Settings → Members. Viewers can read dashboards, editors can change them, and admins manage sources and billing. 

northwind sources add postgres \
  --host db.internal --database analytics --read-only

Dashboards

A dashboard is a grid of tiles. Each tile runs one saved query and refreshes on its own schedule, from every five minutes to once a day. Drag tiles to rearrange them and resize from the corner.

Share a dashboard with a link, embed it in an internal tool with a signed URL, or schedule a PDF snapshot to Slack or email every Monday morning.

  • Filters apply to every tile that uses the same field.
  • Dashboards keep 90 days of version history.
  • Up to 40 tiles per dashboard on the Team plan.

Alerts

Alerts watch a metric and notify you when it crosses a threshold or changes faster than usual. Anomaly alerts learn the normal range from the last 28 days, so give a new metric a few weeks before you rely on them.

Route alerts to Slack, Microsoft Teams, PagerDuty or email. Quiet hours pause non-critical alerts outside your team's working hours.

Plans and billing

The Starter plan is free for up to three members and one source. Team costs €29 per editor per month, with unlimited viewers. Enterprise adds SSO, audit logs and a dedicated success manager.

Invoices are issued on the first of the month. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period; upgrades are prorated immediately.
